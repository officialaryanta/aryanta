I want to apply as seller

You can apply directly from the Aryanta apply link or from Aryanta Store side bar. This guide explains all steps properly.

6 simple apply steps

Complete these steps carefully.

Open The Link

You can open the link by cliking "Open Apply Page" on top or go to the website open side bar clik Sell on Our Platform.

Enter email and phone number

Enter your email and phone number. An OTP will be sent to your email. Enter the OTP and verify your contact.

Enter personal details

Enter your name, address, contact information and required personal details correctly.

Upload Aadhaar, PAN and GST if available

Upload your Aadhaar card image and PAN card image. If you have GST, click I have GST and upload GST. If you do not have GST "SKIP IT" , continue to next step.

Enter business details

Enter your shop name, business category, business details and store information. This helps users see your store on Aryanta.

Enter bank details

Enter correct bank details to receive payment for your product orders. Check account number and IFSC before submitting.

Review and submit

Review everything you uploaded and entered. Check images, personal details, business details and bank details. Then click Submit. After submit, you will see the application uploaded/authorized message.

✓

After application submit

Your seller application will go under review. After verification is complete, Aryanta Team- will send your PIN/account access details to your registered email, SMS or other communication method.

Open the Aryanta Seller Page. Login with the email ID and password sent by the company on your registered mail. Visit your Seller Panel and open My Inventory. From there you can see all added products, edit price, stock, images and description, or delete products if needed. Open Seller Panel and click Low Stock Items. This section shows products with less stock, generally below 10 quantity. You can edit the quantity directly from there and the stock updates immediately without waiting for QC again. Keep stock updated so customers do not order unavailable items.
Open Seller Panel and click New Orders. Here you will see new orders assigned to your store. Click Accept to accept the order. If you do not accept within 32 hours, a fine can be generated on your account. Repeating this more than 5 times may lead to seller ID suspension.
After accepting an order, open Accepted Orders from the side bar and generate the slip/invoice. Once the slip is generated, the courier team will normally visit your pickup address within 48 hours to collect the item. After the item is delivered and settlement is completed, payment will be added to your given bank account. If you accept the order but do not generate the invoice/slip within 12 hours, a fine may be counted. Repeating this more than 5 times can lead to seller ID suspension.
Breached shows orders where seller action was missed. For example, the order was not accepted on time, or the slip/invoice was not generated on time, so the order got cancelled or marked for fine. This page helps you see the product, order details and the specific fine reason.
